This Privacy Policy describes how Lendasat ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use this website and our services. By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

##### What We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. This website does **not** run product analytics, session recording, advertising trackers, or behavioral profiling. In particular, we do not use third-party analytics platforms to record or replay your browsing sessions.

The information processed when you use this website is limited to:

- **Consent preferences** — your cookie choices, stored locally in your browser (see our [Cookie Policy](/faq/legal/lendasat/cookies)). These never leave your device.
- **Region signal** — a first-party `geo-eu` cookie indicating whether your request originates from the EU/EEA/UK, used only to decide whether to display the cookie consent banner.
- **Technical and security data** — standard request information (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps) processed transiently by our hosting and content-delivery provider to serve pages, protect against abuse, and maintain availability.

##### Service Providers

We use **Cloudflare** as our content delivery network and for security and DDoS protection. In providing these services, Cloudflare may process technical request data, including IP addresses, on our behalf. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacy/.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

##### Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors in the EU/EEA/UK, our legal basis for processing includes our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website, and your consent where consent is required (for example, for non-essential functional cookies). You may withdraw consent at any time via the cookie banner or by clearing the consent values stored in your browser.

##### Your Rights

For EU/EEA/UK residents under GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data, request rectification of inaccurate data, request erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restrict or object to processing, request portability, and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents under the CCPA have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, to request deletion of personal information, to opt out (we do not sell personal information), and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@lendasat.com. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.

##### Children

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance.
