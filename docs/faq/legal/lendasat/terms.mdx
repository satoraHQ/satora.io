(d) **Refunds are paid in the asset you locked, not the asset you started with.** If you converted a stablecoin or another asset not denominated in Bitcoin into WBTC or tBTC on the client side before funding a swap (Section 3(c)), and that swap fails, the refund is paid in WBTC or tBTC respectively.
