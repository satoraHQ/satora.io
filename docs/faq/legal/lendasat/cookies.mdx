##### What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the owners of the site. Some sites also use related browser storage, such as `localStorage`, for similar purposes.

##### How We Use Cookies

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum. This website does **not** use analytics, advertising, or session-recording cookies. We only use:

- **Necessary cookies**: required for the website to function and to remember your privacy choices. These are always active.
- **Functional cookies**: optional cookies that remember preferences to provide enhanced functionality. These are only set with your consent.

##### Cookies and Storage We Use

**First-party cookies**

- **geo-eu**: indicates whether your request originates from the EU/EEA/UK so we know whether to display the cookie consent banner. Set by our middleware based on the request's country, with a duration of one day.

**Browser storage (localStorage)**

Your consent choice is stored locally in your browser and never sent to our servers:

- **cookie-consent**: records whether you accepted or declined (`accepted` / `declined`).
- **cookie-preferences**: records which categories you enabled (necessary and, optionally, functional).

**Third-party cookies**

- **Cloudflare**: our content delivery and security provider may set cookies necessary for performance and protection against abuse.

##### Cookie Banner

If you visit from the EU/EEA/UK and have not yet made a choice, you will see a cookie consent banner that lets you:

- **Accept**: enables necessary and functional cookies.
- **Manage**: choose individual categories (necessary is always on; functional is optional).
- **Decline**: only necessary cookies are used.

Your preference is stored locally and respected on subsequent visits.

##### Changing Your Preference

Because your choice is stored in your browser's `localStorage` (not in a regular cookie), clearing only cookies will not reset it. To change or reset your preference, clear your browser's site data (or the `cookie-consent` and `cookie-preferences` values for this site) and reload the page, which will allow the banner to appear again where applicable.

##### Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies and site data through their settings. You can view stored cookies, delete some or all of them, block cookies, and set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being set. Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of this website. Helpful guides:

- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
